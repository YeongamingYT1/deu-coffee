SCENIC & CINEMATIC

Nature, silence, and exceptional coffee above the mist.

Perched high in the Dago hills, this branch delivers an unmatched visual and sensory escape. Designed with open-air terraces and wide glass panes, every seat frames the vast Bandung cityscape and surrounding pine valleys.

Whether you are seeking solitude during quiet morning hours or a breathtaking sunset backdrop for evening catch-ups, Dago transforms your coffee ritual into a cinematic experience.
