THE PHILOSOPHY

Three Places, Three Atmospheres, One De.u Experience.

Rooted in Bandung, crafted for modern lifestyle.

Born from a desire for more than just a quick cup.

De.u Coffee started with a simple belief: coffee is about the space it creates in your day. Rather than forcing every location into a single cookie-cutter template, we tailored each branch to its environment—from the scenic hills of Dago to the energetic neighborhood hangout and the refined urban luxury of Mekarwangi.
